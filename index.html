CONSTELLATION COVERAGE · ILLUSTRATION
Master's thesis · Institute FAPS, FAU · since 03/2026

ML-enabled digital twin for satellite constellation design

Surrogate models that predict European satellite coverage (45–70° N) in milliseconds instead of running slow physics simulations.

  • Modular Python pipeline with OrbitPy and EO-Sim (J2 orbit propagation), NumPy and Pandas to generate and manage large simulation datasets.
  • Training configurations sampled with Latin Hypercube Sampling; targets: coverage percentage, maximum gap and mean revisit time.
  • XGBoost and neural network models trained and evaluated in Docker/WSL with scikit-learn and Git.
  • Integrated into digital-twin workflows for design optimisation; validation planned with Iridium and OneWeb TLE data.
